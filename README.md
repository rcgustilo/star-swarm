# Star Swarm

An original retro space shooter for Android in the spirit of classic arcade "swarm" shooters.

- Alien squadrons fly in on looping paths, settle into formation, then peel off and dive at you.
- **Drones** (cyan), **Raiders** (red) and **Commanders** (green, two hits). Diving aliens score double.
- Commanders can lock a **tractor beam** on your fighter. If yours is captured, shoot that commander while it dives to win the fighter back and fly **two ships side by side** with double firepower.
- Every 4th stage (3, 7, 11, ...) is a **Challenging Stage**: no enemy fire, 100 points per hit, 10,000 for a perfect 40.
- Extra fighter at 20,000 points and every 70,000 after.
- **Controls:** drag anywhere to move; keep your finger down to fire. Pause button top right; Back also pauses.
- High score saved; sound on/off on the title screen.

The APK is built by GitHub Actions on every push and published under Releases.
